Case Studies & Project Outcomes

Real projects. Measurable outcomes. Here's what we've delivered for manufacturers across India.

Tier-1 Automotive Supplier · Pune Automotive

AI Vision Defect Detection on Stamping Line

Deployed a real-time deep learning inspection system on a high-speed metal stamping line. Replaced manual visual QC with a Basler camera + NVIDIA Jetson edge AI setup integrated directly into the existing Siemens PLC reject logic.

Reduced defect escape rate by 94% in 3 months
AI Vision Siemens S7-1500 NVIDIA Jetson PyTorch
Mid-size Press Shop · Aurangabad Manufacturing

Smart OEE Dashboard & Predictive Maintenance System

Built a full IIoT layer using OPC-UA and MQTT to pull live data from 18 machines into a Grafana OEE dashboard. Added vibration-based predictive maintenance alerts that reduced unplanned downtime by flagging bearing wear 5 days in advance.

OEE improved from 61% to 83% within 6 months
OPC-UA MQTT Grafana Predictive Maint.
Industrial Utility Plant · Maharashtra Energy

Legacy PLC Migration & SCADA Modernisation

Migrated a 15-year-old Siemens S5 system to S7-1500 TIA Portal with zero production downtime using a parallel-run cutover strategy. Replaced aging SCADA panels with a modern WinCC Unified web-based HMI accessible from tablets on the shop floor.

Eliminated 3 annual production stoppages, saving ₹18L/yr
