Our Story

Built by Engineers,
For Engineers.

MordeTech Solutions was born from one engineer's belief that Indian manufacturing deserves world-class automation — accessible, intelligent, and built to last.

Priyanka Morde
Founder & Director

An automation engineer with over 13+ years of hands-on experience in Siemens PLC systems, machine vision, and IIoT architectures. Passionate about bringing AI to the factory floor.

Siemens-Trained Engineers AI / ML IIoT Expert

The Founder's Vision

Priyanka Morde started MordeTech Solutions in 2012 with a simple but powerful mission: to make world-class industrial automation accessible to Indian manufacturers — not just the large corporations, but the mid-size factories that form the backbone of India's industrial economy.

Having worked on the shop floor as a controls engineer, Priyanka understood firsthand the gap between what automation technology could deliver and what most manufacturers were actually experiencing. She saw PLCs programmed in the 1990s still running critical production lines. She saw operators making decisions without real data. She saw AI and machine vision as distant aspirations rather than practical tools.

"Every factory deserves a brain. Our job is to give it one — intelligently, reliably, and affordably."
— Priyanka Morde, Founder & Director

Today, MordeTech Solutions operates across automotive, manufacturing, energy, and smart factory sectors — having delivered 150+ projects across India and internationally. The mission remains the same: engineer intelligence into every factory.
